# Indicator comparison, historical research only

BTCUSDT Binance 1h closes, 2026-09-01 to 2026-11-01 UTC, 1,464 bars; first 50 bars warm-up. Strict crossing of CCI14 zero (205 events), Wilder RSI14 through 50 (149), and close through SMA20 (164); these are research rules, not original MT5 indicator implementations. Each event has subsequent 1/4/24-bar close-price directional comparisons where available. Signals computed from bars up to and including their own closed bar; tests check future-bar changes do not change preceding events. Common evaluation window and same bar series. First-bar RSI seed and exact equality-cross treatment are specified by the code and may differ from MT5.

All outcomes are gross hypothetical directional close-to-close changes, NOT executable returns, trade P&L or evidence of profitability; signal-close fills unavailable in general. No spread, fees, slippage, latency, rollover, exposure overlap, confidence intervals, time-varying market regime, multiple testing correction or realistic portfolio logic. Sample selection and source data rights unknown. 24-bar horizon overlaps heavily. Clock disagreement persists for retrieval date; selected dataset interval precedes trusted session date. Date/price timestamps are those claimed by the source, not independently verified. No live tracking, no orders. Sample values may change at source and adapters are not compared to MT5.

The comparison script checks page hashes and verifies saved source rows against their first page and contiguous hourly bars, but no independent source certification. The entire event list and outcome numbers are in the private comparison JSON, with input SQLite checkpoint backed up separately.
