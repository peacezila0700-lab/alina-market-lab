- **Date:** 2026-11-20 · **Status:** COMPLETED → **H05 REJECTED** (3 of 4 pre-registered criteria triggered)
- **Pre-registration:** `docs/PREREG_H05.md` committed **before** the run (`ab4d231`)

## 4. Why the fade rule loses (diagnostics)

- In-sample expectancy is **negative** (−1.66 pips over 114 trades, PF 0.80) — the rule loses
  precisely where it has the most data. Validation and OOS being mildly positive is noise.
- Exit mix in-sample: day_flat 56, ma_cross 35, **stop_loss 23**. The stop is the leak:
  fading an intraday spike gets run over before the reversion shows up.
- EURUSD flips sign across blocks (−4.6 → −9.1 → +5.52) with the same rule → the "signal"
  is not a property of the market, it is a property of the sample.

## 5. Honest limits of this experiment

- 2 years of H1 only (vendor ceiling), 2–3 regimes.
- No measured spread: at 1.3 pips assumed. Real spread is likely wider at 21:00 UTC rollover,
  which is exactly where the forced-flat exit happens — this could make results *worse*, not better.
- Yahoo H1 has 295/318 null bars removed and 149/152 gaps > 3× the median step.
