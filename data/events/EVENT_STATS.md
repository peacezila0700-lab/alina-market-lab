## Reading rules

- The `|move| ratio` column is the honest test of 'is this event special': the median event day should be visibly larger than a random day, otherwise the event only *sounds* important.
- For directional claims compare the median against the unconditional median at the same window and require a big gap plus a consistent sign across assets.
- `NFP_approx` dates are rule-derived (first Friday) and ~90-95% exact: treat its numbers as indicative. CPI is deliberately absent — BLS blocks this sandbox and a wrong date would poison the study.
- `VIX_ONSET30` / `SHOCK_2.5SIG` cannot be known before the close, so their forward window starts the next day. Do not 'buy the shock day close' in a backtest.
