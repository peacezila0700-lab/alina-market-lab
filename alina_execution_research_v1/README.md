# More realistic directional research proxy, v1

Experimental historical backtest, NOT live trades, BTCUSDT spot OHLC 1h, Aug 1 to Nov 22 2026 UTC exclusive (2,712 bars). Each CCI14 zero, RSI14 through 50, and close/SMA20 strict crossover uses the same 50-bar warmup and signal is computed after its candle closes. Buy/sell is a hypothetical long/short direction; spot data does not establish shorting access. Assuming entry at immediately next candle open still assumes zero-latency ideal fill and may be unrealistic. Exit at closes 1, 4, 24 bars later. 0, 10 and 20 basis points roundtrip are hypothetical fixed deductions, NOT actual fees, spreads or slippage; ignores market impact, funding, borrowing, compounding and position overlaps. Each signal measured independently, not a portfolio. No out-of-sample tests or significance estimates; no verified MT5 equivalence or data rights. Dates in historical source are provider-claimed; retrieval clock is disputed, sample ends before trusted current date. No real orders or recommendations.

For the 1-bar horizon, in this sample average directional move before costs was approximately CCI 0.0306%, RSI 0.0369%, SMA 0.0254%; with hypothetical 10 bps roundtrip subtracted they become -0.0694%, -0.0631%, -0.0746% respectively. This is sample-specific and should not be extrapolated.

`python3 -m unittest -v` uses synthetic data. `execution_eval.py --db <sqlite> --output <new-json>` verifies page SHA and raw rows via comparison build, checks continuity and writes no-overwrite research file. Its dependency is ../alina_catchup_v1/compare_replay.py. Full input data and results included only in private checkpoints; do not publish source data without rights review.
