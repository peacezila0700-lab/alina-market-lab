is the signature of **noise being fitted by a rule** — not a market mechanism.

Consequences recorded:

## 6. What was actually learned (this is the deliverable)

1. **The range behaviour is real; the D1 z-score rule is not a way to trade it.**
   Hurst 0.36, VR60 0.52, 66% of days within ±2% of MA200 — all true, and all still true after this
   experiment. Description ≠ exploitation.
2. **Costs were never the binding constraint** (1.3 pips vs ~150+ pip stops). The binding constraints
   are sample size and regime dependence.
3. **Rule freezes work.** Because the threshold was never tuned, this "no" is trustworthy. A tuned
   version would have produced a prettier, worthless answer.
4. **Yahoo D1 history is 26 years deep but needs the R1–R4 repairs**: 4,186 logged repairs across
   the basket (796 Sunday→Friday relabels on EURGBP alone).
