## How to use this

1. If a rule must trade, restrict it to the hours with the highest `range / spread`; EXP-0018 showed that gating by hour alone does not create an edge, it only reduces the cost of one.
2. The 21-22Z and 00-02Z hours are the rollover window: spreads widen several-fold and the range shrinks. Any hourly strategy should simply be flat there.
3. XAUUSD's spread is ~39 pips (0.39 USD) — gold is a *daily* instrument at retail cost; hourly gold ideas need ~80 pips of movement just to break even on a round trip.
